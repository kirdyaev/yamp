Yamamp requires your personal OAuth token stored in macOS Keychain (dev.kirdyaev.yamamp) to load Search, Playlists, «Мне нравится», and «Моя волна».

Playback controls (Play, Pause, Next, Prev, Volume, EQ) are handled directly inside the Winamp player on the left.

Your token is stored exclusively in macOS Keychain and is never written to disk, logs, or git.
